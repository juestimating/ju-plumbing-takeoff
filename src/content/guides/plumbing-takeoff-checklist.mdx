A plumbing takeoff checklist is the list of what a takeoff must cover, system by system, so nothing slips between the first sheet and the final total. Use it twice on every bid: before you measure, to settle the documents and scope questions in the first group, and after, to confirm that every item in each system group has a takeoff line with a sheet reference or a written exclusion. Each group closes with a **Watch for** line naming its most common miss, and the last section settles who provides the items that fall between trades.

## Plumbing takeoff checklist: documents and scope questions

- **Plumbing sheets** match the sheet index, including every riser diagram, isometric, detail and schedule.
- **Other disciplines:** architectural, structural and civil sheets, plus the mechanical, kitchen and fire protection sheets that call for plumbing connections.
- **Specifications:** Division 22 and the related sections in 21, 23, 31 and 33, with the [bid instructions](/guides/plumbing-bid-checklist).
- **Addenda,** logged by number and date, with every revised sheet identified.
- **Scale** calibrated on each sheet and confirmed with a second dimension.
- **Legend, abbreviations and general notes** read before any counting starts.
- **Scope questions** settled before you measure, using the trade-split table at the end of this checklist.

**Watch for:** an addendum that revises plumbing sheets without changing the sheet index, so compare the revision dates printed on the sheets.

## Fixtures, trim and equipment: what to count

**Fixtures and trim,** counted from the [fixture schedule](/guides/how-to-read-plumbing-plans) and confirmed on the plans:

- **Fixtures by tag** (EA), reconciled between the schedule and the plans, floor by floor.
- **Accessible variants,** counted separately from standard units.
- **Carriers and supports** for wall-hung closets, urinals and lavatories.
- **Flush valves, faucets and trim,** by the schedule's model or basis of design.
- **Stops, supplies and traps** for every lavatory, sink and similar fixture.
- **Protective covers** on exposed supplies and drains under accessible lavatories and sinks.
- **Mixing valves,** thermostatic or tempering, at fixtures or groups where specified.
- **Water hammer arrestors** at fixture groups, where drawn or specified.
- **Trap primers** for floor drains and floor sinks, where drawn or specified.
- **Food-service connections:** indirect waste and supplies for dishwashers, ice machines and similar units.

**Equipment,** counted from the equipment schedule:

- **Water heaters** (EA): storage, tankless and commercial, with the flue or vent included or carried by others.
- **Boilers and heat exchangers,** only if they sit in plumbing scope.
- **Pumps:** recirculation, booster, sump and ejector, with basins and controls.
- **Expansion tanks, master mixing valves and relief valves,** with the relief valve discharge piping.
- **Water treatment:** softeners and filters.
- **Interceptors:** grease, oil, solids or sand, with excavation for in-ground units.
- **Backflow assemblies** by type and size.
- **Connections at every unit:** isolation valves, unions, flexible connectors, gauges and drains.
- **Pads, supports and seismic anchors.**
- **Condensate drains** from high-efficiency heaters.
- **Start-up, training and manuals,** where specified.

**Watch for:** owner-furnished fixtures, which still need rough-in, setting and connection labor; connections shown only on the kitchen equipment drawings; and relief valve discharge, condensate drains and equipment pads, which sit between plumbing, mechanical and general trades.

## Sanitary waste and vent piping: what to measure

- **Pipe by size and material** (LF): horizontal mains and branches, stacks, vent headers, and individual and branch vents.
- **Above-slab and below-slab piping,** each in its own type.
- **Vertical runs** from riser diagrams, isometrics or floor-to-floor heights, including the run from each fixture to the horizontal piping.
- **Fittings by type, size and material** (EA), from isometrics or from plan counts plus a [documented allowance](/guides/plumbing-pipe-waste-and-fitting-allowances).
- **Couplings** on hubless cast iron, counted apart from the fittings.
- **Cleanouts** by type (floor, wall, grade) and size, with covers and boxes.
- **Floor drains, floor sinks and funnel drains,** with strainers and trap primer connections.
- **Special waste:** grease waste, acid waste and indirect waste, each as its own system.
- **Roof penetrations** for vents, with flashing.
- **Air admittance valves,** where drawn.

**Watch for:** vents. Each carries little pipe and joins where a crowded plan hides it, so trace every vent from the fixture to the roof or a stack.

## Domestic hot, cold and recirculation water items

- **Service entrance:** meter and box where drawn, backflow preventer, pressure-reducing valve, strainer, main shutoff and bypass.
- **Pipe by size and material** (LF): cold, hot and hot water return as separate runs, from mains and risers to branch mains and fixture branches.
- **Fittings and joints** by type, size and joining method, as the specification sets it.
- **Valves** (EA): isolation, check, pressure-reducing, hose bibbs and wall hydrants.
- **Expansion control and water hammer arrestors,** where drawn.
- **Recirculation:** balancing valves and, where specified, heat trace, with the pump listed under equipment.

Insulation, hangers and testing for the water system are listed in the last two groups.

**Watch for:** size changes. Each size is its own run, and each riser steps down as it rises, so carrying the ground-floor size to the roof overstates the large pipe.

## Storm drainage and roof drain items to take off

- **Roof drains and overflow drains** (EA), by type and size from the schedule, with sump receivers, strainers and extensions.
- **Leaders and horizontal storm piping** (LF) by size and material.
- **Overflow piping,** routed and sized separately where drawn.
- **Cleanouts** on leaders and horizontal runs.
- **Insulation** on horizontal storm piping and drain bodies, where specified.
- **Controlled-flow devices, offsets and expansion fittings,** where drawn.
- **Area, planter and trench drains** that sit in plumbing scope.
- **Sumps and sump pumps,** listed with the equipment.
- **The connection** to the site storm system, and the point where building scope ends.

**Watch for:** the building-to-site boundary and overflow lines that discharge separately. Both are easy to price twice or not at all.

## Gas, medical gas and fire sprinkler piping items

- **Fuel gas:** pipe by size and material (black steel, CSST or polyethylene underground), fittings, shutoff valves, regulators, drip legs, regulator vent lines where drawn, appliance connectors and shutoffs at each unit, and tracer wire and transitions on polyethylene. Sizing is design, so the takeoff takes sizes as drawn.
- **Medical gas:** pipe by gas (oxygen, medical air, vacuum, nitrous oxide and waste anesthetic gas disposal), zone valve boxes, alarm panels, and outlets and inlets by room type. Note where the source equipment boundary sits.
- **Fire sprinkler:** heads by type from the reflected ceiling plan, mains, cross mains and branch lines by size, risers, the fire department connection, and hangers and bracing. Hydraulic calculations are not part of a takeoff.

**Watch for:** gas appliance connections shown only on the mechanical or kitchen sheets. Count them from there, and log any mismatch with the plumbing sheets.

## Underground piping and site connection items

- **Pipe by size and material** (LF), with trench length grouped by depth band and read from invert elevations.
- **Fittings,** cleanouts, boxes and manholes.
- **Bedding and backfill** by material, with compaction.
- **Excavation:** who digs, shores, dewaters, hauls spoil and restores the surface.
- **Thrust blocks or joint restraint** on buried pressure pipe.
- **Tracer wire and warning tape,** where specified.
- **Tie-ins** to the main.
- **Cutting and patching** of existing pavement or slab.
- **Wall and footing penetrations:** sleeves and seals at foundation walls.

**Watch for:** excavation scope. Priced twice, it loses the bid. Missed, it loses money.

## Supports, insulation, firestopping and accessories

- **Hangers and supports** (EA) by pipe size, material and type, using the spacing the specification or governing code sets, with riser clamps at each floor.
- **Seismic bracing,** where the project requires it.
- **Insulation** by service, pipe size and thickness, with jackets, fitting covers and vapor barrier on cold lines, plus inserts or shields at hangers.
- **Firestopping** (EA) at rated floors and walls, by assembly and pipe type.
- **Sleeves and core drilling** (EA) through slabs and walls, with scanning where the structure calls for it.
- **Escutcheons, access panels and pipe identification.**
- **Expansion loops, joints and guides,** where drawn.

**Watch for:** rated assemblies. They are shown on the architectural life-safety and wall-type sheets, not always on the plumbing sheets, so count penetrations from the architectural set.

## Testing, exclusions and trade splits to write down

Close the takeoff with the lines that no drawing shows.

- **Testing:** drain, waste and vent tests, water pressure tests, flushing and disinfection with sampling where required, backflow assembly tests, gas pressure tests, storm tests, and camera inspection of underground sewers where specified.
- **Exclusions:** engineering and design, hydraulic or sizing calculations, code compliance review, excavation if by others, fire sprinkler and medical gas if by others, electrical connections, access panels, abatement of hazardous materials, and any work marked by others.

These items fall between trades. Settle each one in writing before you price:

| Item | Ask who provides it | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| Excavation and backfill | Plumbing, the site contractor or both | Earthwork specification, civil sheets, bid instructions |
| Core drilling, sleeves and patching | Plumbing, a concrete trade or general trades | Bid instructions, structural drawings |
| Concrete pads and housekeeping bases | Plumbing or general trades | Equipment schedule, structural drawings |
| Equipment connections, flues, combustion air and condensate drains | Plumbing or mechanical | Mechanical drawings, equipment schedule |
| Electrical connections and controls | Electrical, with plumbing setting the equipment | Electrical drawings, equipment schedule |
| Gas piping at the meter and regulator | Plumbing, mechanical or the gas utility | Gas plan, civil sheets, bid instructions |
| Roof curbs and flashing at penetrations | Plumbing or roofing | Roofing specification, plumbing details |
| Firestopping | Plumbing or a firestopping subcontractor | Division 07 and 22 specifications |
| Insulation | Plumbing or an insulation subcontractor | Division 22 insulation section |
| Access panels and wall blocking | Plumbing, drywall or general trades | Architectural and Division 22 specifications |
| Fire sprinkler and medical gas | A separate trade or plumbing | Bid instructions, Divisions 21 and 22 |
| Fixtures and equipment | Contractor, owner or others | Schedule remarks |
| Backflow testing and reports | Plumbing or a separate testing firm | Division 22 specification, water utility rules |
| Temporary water | General conditions or plumbing | Division 01, bid instructions |
| Utility tap and capacity fees | Owner, general contractor or plumbing | Bid instructions, civil sheets |
